IMPORTANT FOR YOUR WAMP SETUP
=============================

Your MySQL default_storage_engine was MyISAM. Before running this SMS project, use InnoDB.

1) Open C:\wamp64\...\my.ini and under [mysqld] set:

default-storage-engine=InnoDB

2) Restart WAMP services.

3) In phpMyAdmin run:

SHOW VARIABLES LIKE 'default_storage_engine';

Expected Value: InnoDB

4) In Laravel config/database.php, inside the mysql connection, change:

'engine' => null,

to:

'engine' => 'InnoDB',

5) Then run:

php artisan optimize:clear
php artisan migrate:fresh --seed
php artisan storage:link
php artisan serve
